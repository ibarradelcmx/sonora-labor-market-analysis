# Sonora Labor Market Analysis
### A data-driven look at employment trends and economic opportunities in Northwest Mexico

## Overview
Analysis of Sonora's labor market using official INEGI microdata (ENOE Q4 2025) — 419,038 records covering employment, income, and economic activity across municipalities and sectors.

## Key Findings
- Sonora has a 2.93% unemployment rate, ranking 6th lowest nationally
- Construction leads employment in Hermosillo with 1,513 workers
- Commerce sector pays the highest average income ($18,946 MXN/month)
- Hermosillo outperforms Cajeme by 26% in average monthly income ($14,278 vs $11,330 MXN)
